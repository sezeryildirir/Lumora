Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas of Los Angeles do you serve?
We serve the greater Los Angeles area including Santa Monica, West Hollywood, Beverly Hills, Venice, Pasadena, the Valley and Downtown LA. See our full service-area list — and if you don't see your neighborhood, message us; we can usually accommodate.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Lumora Property Services is fully licensed and insured, and every team member is background-checked and professionally trained before entering a client's home.
What's included in a deep cleaning?
Everything in a standard clean plus baseboards, light fixtures, interior windows, high dusting, kitchen degreasing, cabinet fronts and detailed bathroom sanitation. It's ideal for a first visit or seasonal refresh.
Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
Yes — our teams arrive with professional equipment and eco-friendly, pet-safe products at no extra charge. If you prefer specific products, just let us know.
How do Airbnb and vacation rental turnovers work?
We sync with your booking calendar, clean between stays, reset linens, restock amenities, complete a photo-documented inspection and flag any damage — so every guest walks into a five-star space.
What if I'm not happy with the cleaning?
Tell us within 24 hours and we'll return to re-clean the areas in question, free of charge. Your satisfaction is guaranteed.
